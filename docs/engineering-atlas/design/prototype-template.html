5 · Ship It: plain first, deeper on request

390 px · round 2 of 3

Round 2 starts with the simpler version, where the payment company keeps retrying for days. Four plain choices: two work, one half-works, one makes it worse. "In engineering terms" holds the precise vocabulary. The optional harder twist (the company gives up after an hour) adds a fifth choice and changes which answers still work. The trade-offs stay accurate. A waiting line (durable queue) makes delivery reliable but still processes the duplicate. Only remembering handled payments (idempotency at the processing boundary) stops double charges.

6 · Rendered text contrast audit

computed live from every text element inside the frames above

For each element with its own text, this reads the computed text colour and the first opaque background behind it, blending any translucent layers. It then applies the WCAG 2.x ratio: 4.5:1 for normal text, 3:1 for large text (24 px+, or 18.7 px+ bold). SVG text and text over images are listed as not checked.
