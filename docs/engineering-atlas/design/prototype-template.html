1 · Atlas home, desktop

1440 px · light, then dark

An asymmetric hero opens the page. Role, specialism, selected work and contact are all visible without interacting. The plate on the right maps the real career route over contour lines. The workspace entry is dashed to mark it as optional, a side door rather than the front door.

2 · Atlas home, mobile

390 px · light and dark

Single column with full-width 52 px actions. The menu is a disclosure button. The email address is selectable text with a copy button, because a mailto link alone can silently do nothing.

3 · Project chapter: Spectra

1440 px · /work/spectra/

The chapter layout: a numbered chapter mark, a facts row, a full-bleed screenshot spread, a narrow reading column and a stage-by-stage diagram. Where the evidence is thin, the frame shows a gap honestly instead of filling it.

4 · Ganesh Workspace

/workspace/ · 1440 × 900 and 390 px

A drafting table at night: slate wallpaper, faint contours, paper windows. The windows reuse the same content views as the Atlas pages. The focused window has a cobalt title bar. In the dock, Projects is focused, Terminal is open in the background and Journey is minimized (hollow dot).

5 · Ship It storyboard

390 px · round 2 of 3 (payment webhook, variant B)

Turn based: choose, then confirm. Confirm stays disabled until an option is selected, so a double tap cannot submit twice. There are no timers. Every number is labelled illustrative. Frames 3 and 4 show the key lesson. A durable queue makes processing reliable but still processes duplicates. Only idempotency at the processing boundary removes them.
